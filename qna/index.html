ASSESSMENT CRITERIA

Five areas of understanding.

The assessment covers individual contribution, reasoning about the method, evaluation, limitations and the ability to adapt the approach.

01 · CONTRIBUTION

Work completed

Identify the notebook sections, code, analysis or tests you led. Explain the problem addressed, the decision made and the evidence produced.

02 · KEY CHOICES

Method and assumptions

Explain the 16-day information boundary, shifted sales lags, log1p target, train-only preprocessing, seasonal-naive fallback and LightGBM configuration.

03 · EVALUATION

Results and metrics

Distinguish the three rolling-validation folds from the separate final holdout. Explain why RMSLE selects the model and why MAE can rank models differently.

04 · LIMITATION

Limits of the evidence

Discuss the 80,000-row fitting cap, known-promotion assumption, three validation folds, one retailer and the absence of calibrated forecast intervals.

05 · SMALL CHANGE

Reasoning about a change

Explain how a longer horizon, unavailable promotions, a new store or a different evaluation metric would alter features, validation or conclusions.
