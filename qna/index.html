A focused preparation guide for the five-point individual assessment. Each student should explain their own work, key choices, evaluation, a limitation and the reasoning behind a small change.

Assessment criterion · 5 points: Each student explains their contribution, key choices, evaluation, and a limitation, and reasons about a small change.
WHAT THE EXAMINER IS LOOKING FOR

Five things each student must be able to explain.

The defense is individual. Be prepared to describe the notebook, code, evidence and decisions in your own words.

01 · CONTRIBUTION

What did you personally do?

Identify the actual notebook cells, code, analysis, website pages or testing you personally completed. Explain one concrete decision you made and how you checked it.

02 · KEY CHOICES

Why these methods?

Explain the 16-day information boundary, shifted sales lags, log1p target, train-only preprocessing, Seasonal naive (Base model) and why LightGBM was compared.

03 · EVALUATION

What does the evidence say?

Explain the three rolling folds, separate final holdout, RMSLE and MAE, training-row cap, and why the preferred model depends on the metric.

04 · LIMITATION

What weakens the claim?

Discuss a real limitation: 80,000-row fitting cap, known-promotion assumption, single retailer/history, three validation folds, or lack of calibrated forecast intervals.

05 · SMALL CHANGE

What if one condition changed?

Reason through how a changed horizon, unavailable promotions, new store or different evaluation metric would affect features, validation, deployment or conclusions.
